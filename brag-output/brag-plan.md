# LabBridge — launch video plan

**What it is:** a first-person 3D science lab in the browser where you do the practical with your own hands, and an AI lab manager (Dr. Curie) watches and marks you.
**For:** students without access to a real lab (and their teachers).
**Sets it apart:** nothing is a button that plays an animation. You pour, pipette, turn the tap, swirl and read the meniscus yourself, and the sheet you write is what's marked.
**Most impressive claim:** parallax is real: read the burette from above and you get it wrong.
**Visual hook:** the gloved hands doing a real titration, flask turning pink.
**Share caption:** "No lab? Do the titration anyway."

**Angle:** the app's own problem statement → the real thing, hands-on, step by step.
**Tone:** default (punchy, clean). Landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 21 s.
**Identity:** the app's slate-950 ground, emerald → teal → cyan gradient, white bold sans; real screenshots of the running app, with its own step names as eyebrows.
